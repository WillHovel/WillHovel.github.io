2. Component Datasheets & Search Strategy

Keep these four official datasheets open in your browser tabs:

  • Microcontroller (U1): ESP32-S3-MINI-1 Datasheet
  • 3.3V LDO Voltage Regulator (U2): AP2112K-3.3 Datasheet

  • 3.3V CAN Bus Transceiver (U4, U6, U8, U10, U11): SN65HVD230 Datasheet

How to Skim Datasheets (Without Reading 90 Pages)

Datasheets look intimidating because they contain dozens of pages of timing diagrams, register maps, and mechanical tolerances. Never read a datasheet front-to-back. Use Ctrl+F and jump directly to these key sections:

  • Features & Description (Page 1): Quick summary of what the device does, its operating voltage range, and maximum speed/current.
  • Pin Definitions / Pin Configuration: A table listing what every physical pin does and whether it is active-high or active-low (often indicated by an overline or slash like /CS or /RESET, meaning LOW / 0V triggers the action).
  • Recommended Operating Conditions & Electrical Characteristics: Safe voltage, logic thresholds, and current limits.
  • Typical Application / Peripheral Circuit Schematics: Example schematics showing how the chip manufacturer connects capacitors, pull-up resistors, and crystal oscillators.

Spoiler Clue Rule: Each question below lists the general Datasheet Section where the answer lives so you can practice finding numbers. If you've spent 5+ minutes searching and are completely stuck, an Emergency Page Clue is hidden in invisible white text between the brackets. Click and drag your mouse across the space between the brackets to select/highlight it and reveal the page number!

3. Quick Primer: KiCad Schematic Symbols

Here is a quick cheat sheet for the KiCad notations used across our Control Board:

KiCad ElementSchematic NotationWhat It Means
Reference DesignatorsU1, R7, C4, D1, J5, S1, PWR2Unique part ID on the board: U = Integrated Circuit (chip), R = Resistor, C = Capacitor, D = Diode, J = Connector/Header, S = Switch/Button.
Component Values10u, 100n, .1u, 5.1k, 120Physical component value: u = microfarads (µF), n = nanofarads (nF; remember 100 nF = 0.1 µF), k = kilo-ohms (kΩ), bare number = ohms (Ω).
Global LabelsPentagon-shaped label tags (e.g., 5V, 3v3, D+, MOSI-A, CS-A1)Virtual wires that connect nets across the entire project (even between different schematic sheets) without drawing messy wires across the screen. Any pins attached to the exact same global label name are connected together.
Power Symbols & PWR_FLAGUpward bars (5V, 3v3), ground symbols (GND), and PWR_FLAGConnect to power and ground planes. PWR_FLAG is a special KiCad marker telling the Electrical Rules Checker (ERC) "this net is intentionally supplied by an external power source," preventing false warnings when power enters through passive connectors or diodes.
Hierarchical SheetGreen rectangular box labeled CAN SPI (can.kicad_sch)A modular schematic sub-sheet (like a function or module in software). It keeps the top-level schematic uncluttered while containing the detailed multi-channel CAN circuits.
No-Connect Flag (X)Small blue X on unused pinsTells KiCad ERC that a pin is intentionally left unconnected / floating, not forgotten.
