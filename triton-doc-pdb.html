2. Component Datasheets & Search Strategy

In real hardware engineering, someone won't always hand you a pre-made list of PDF links—you often have to look up parts from a schematic yourself.

How to Find Datasheets from a KiCad Schematic

  • Inspect Component Properties in KiCad: Hover over any part on the schematic and press E (or double-click it) to open its Symbol Properties. Look at the Value field (which usually holds the Manufacturer Part Number, or MPN) and the Datasheet field (which often has a URL pre-filled).
  • Decode Generic Suffixes & Packaging Codes:
  • Placeholder letters (xx): On Sheet 1, the diodes D1–D4 are labeled 1.5KExxCA. Here, 1.5KE is the series name, xx is a placeholder for the specific breakdown voltage rating we order (e.g., 1.5KE51CA or 1.5KE68CA), and CA means bidirectional. Searching "1.5KE Vishay datasheet" brings up the entire family datasheet.
  • Ordering/Packaging suffixes (#TRPBF, TR): On Sheet 2, IC2 is labeled LTC3810IG#TRPBF. The base part number is LTC3810 (I = industrial temperature range, G = 28-lead SSOP package, #TRPBF = Tape & Reel, Lead-Free). Always search using the base part number ("LTC3810 datasheet").
  • Where to Search: Type "<Base Part Number> datasheet pdf" into Google, or search the part number directly on DigiKey or Mouser and click the Datasheet link on the product page.

Use the steps above to practice looking up the five main parts on the PDB, and use these verified links to check your finds:

  • Bidirectional TVS Protection Diodes (D1, D2, D3, D4): Vishay 1.5KE Series (1.5KExxCA) Datasheet
  • 100V Synchronous Buck Controller (IC2): Analog Devices LTC3810 Datasheet
  • 100V N-Channel Power Trench MOSFETs (IC1, IC3): onsemi FDD86102 Datasheet
  • 100V SOT-23 N-Channel Pass MOSFET (M1): Diodes Inc. ZXMN10A07F Datasheet
  • 100V 1A Schottky Barrier Diodes (D5, D6): ROHM RB168MM100 Datasheet

How to Skim Datasheets (Without Reading 90 Pages)

Datasheets look intimidating because they contain dozens of pages of timing diagrams, characteristic curves, and mechanical tolerances. Never read a datasheet front-to-back. Use Ctrl+F and jump directly to these key sections:

  • Features, Description & Typical Application (Page 1): Quick summary of what the device does, its voltage/current ratings, and an example reference schematic.
  • Pin Functions / Pin Configuration: A list explaining what every physical pin does and how it should be wired.
  • Absolute Maximum Ratings & Electrical Characteristics: Safe voltage limits, on-resistance (rDS(on)), reference voltages (VFB), and threshold voltages.
  • Operation & Applications Information: Detailed explanations of how the control loop, gate drivers, current sensing, and component sizing work.

Here is a quick cheat sheet for the KiCad notations used across our Power Distribution Board:

Component Values1000u, 270u, 0.1u, 1n, 47p, 10u, 261kPhysical value: u = microfarads (µF) for capacitors or microhenries (µH) for inductors (L1), n = nanofarads (nF; 1n = 1000 pF), p = picofarads (pF), k = kilo-ohms (kΩ).
Net LabelsText labels sitting directly on a wire (e.g., SW, TG, BG, VFB, NDRV, ITH, VOUT)Local virtual wires within the same schematic sheet. Any wires on that sheet with the exact same label name are electrically connected together without drawing cluttering lines across the page.
Power SymbolsVDD, +44V, +12V, GNDGlobal power and ground rails. Every +44V symbol across both sheets connects to the same 44V bus, and every GND symbol connects to the common ground plane.
Hierarchical SheetRectangular box labeled Buck Converter (Buck_Converter.kicad_sch)A modular sub-sheet containing the 44V-to-12V step-down converter circuitry, keeping the main PDB sheet clean.

Part 3: Gate Drive Power & High-Side Bootstrap Circuit (Buck_Converter.kicad_sch)

MOSFET gates need around 10V of gate-to-source voltage (VGS) to turn on fully, which is much lower than 44V (so applying 44V directly to a gate would destroy it!), yet for the top MOSFET (IC1), its source pin (SW) jumps all the way up to +44V when it turns on. Let's see how the LTC3810 solves both problems.
